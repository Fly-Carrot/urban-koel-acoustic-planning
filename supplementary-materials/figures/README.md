## Accessibility

- **S1:** station-fold locations and recording-effort coverage through time.
- **S2-S3:** calibration of daily-detection and potential-host models at withheld stations.
- **S4-S6:** coefficient uncertainty, spatial-transfer diagnostics and linked acoustic-activity components.
- **S7-S8:** city prediction support and overlap with planning-sensitive urban contexts.
- **S9-S10:** monitoring-priority surfaces and ranked candidate sequences.
