# Asian koel audio examples

Two recordings of Asian koel (*Eudynamys scolopaceus*) from the study's passive acoustic monitoring in Shenzhen, China. The song and call labels were supplied by the study authors.

| Example | Recording date and time (China Standard Time) | Original WAV |
|---|---|---|
| Call | 13 May 2024, 04:30 | [BJS_20240513_043000_CALL.WAV](BJS_20240513_043000_CALL.WAV) |
| Song | 22 June 2025, 17:20 | [XL_20250622_172000_SONG.WAV](XL_20250622_172000_SONG.WAV) |

Each file is 60 seconds long, mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Files are shared unchanged as provided by the study authors. Open a file and choose **Raw** or **Download** to play or save it, depending on your browser.

These two selected recordings are provided as listening examples; the full recording archive is not distributed here. They illustrate vocalizations and do not quantify residents' perceived disturbance.
