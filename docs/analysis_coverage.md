# What can be reproduced?

| Part | Included and checked | Not repeated in this update |
|---|---|---|
| Survey inputs | Actual responses/missing masks, 68 dated weeks, daily counts, five folds | Recognition from raw audio |
| Host and Koel fits | Full and train/test inputs at 250 m; retained standardised designs; verified seeds/settings where available | Every full MCMC fit or historical search |
| Density | Actual HEQZ/S1 and D1 design/count inputs | Full S1 refitting: seed unverified |
| City prediction | All 713,128 cell-week summaries from 600 retained weekly-model draws | Raster extraction/geometry reconstruction |
| Planning | All 2,700 group-draw summaries from joint HEQZ and D1 | Residents’ experience or complaints |
| Support | All 13,714 cells; PASS + Boundary remains Primary Results | Spatial geometry |
| Monitoring | Both sequences, updated support, spacing, nested 5/10/15-site portfolios | Future field performance |

Fitting uses dated annual phases with a 365.2425-day period. The retained city prototype deliberately uses a 52-week projection cycle. These bases serve different steps and are not silently substituted.

Planning uses the opportunity component of the **joint HEQZ model**, not the separate H4 weekly-map coefficients. Selected draws reproduce summaries; they are not full chains for a fresh convergence assessment.

Coordinate-trend benchmarks, Moran diagnostics and 100/500 m comparisons remain available as supplementary results, but their complete inputs/refits are outside this de-identified export.
