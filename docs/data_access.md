# Data access

## Included

- Current SA supplementary PDF, 17 workbooks, 10 supplementary figures, main figures and Table 1.
- Actual de-identified [model-ready inputs](../data/model-inputs/README.md).
- Selected [coefficient draws/reference results](../data/model-outputs/README.md).
- Summary CSVs, synthetic examples, R functions and Stan models.

ST01–ST31 are consistent across responses, folds and predictors. Random cell codes match across predictions, planning and monitoring. Missing surveys remain missing; dates and numeric predictors remain real.

These are de-identified, not fully anonymised, data. No identity key is published. Do not attempt to identify recorder locations.

## Not included

Exact coordinates, private identity maps, raw recordings, granular POI records, source rasters, AI weights and full chains. Unfinished social-media data, the main manuscript and author-information files are not distributed.

Candidate conflict pairs permit the retained spacing test without coordinates. They do not support independent map reconstruction or spatial-residual diagnostics.

Sources/licences are in [source notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and Supplementary Data 7 and 15. OSM-derived planning weights are ODbL 1.0, separate from project-owned data.
