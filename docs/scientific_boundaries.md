# Scientific interpretation boundaries

1. **Acoustic opportunity is vocal evidence.** Weekly `psi` does not identify abundance, nesting, continuous residence or general habitat use.
2. **Potential hosts are contextual predictors.** Acoustic co-occurrence does not confirm host choice or parasitism in Shenzhen.
3. **Detection is part of inference.** Recording effort and acoustic event rates enter `p`; they are not ecological effects on `psi`.
4. **Exposure is design-specific.** `E` is the probability that a scheduled sampled minute is classified Koel-positive under the study design. It is not sound level, perceived loudness, annoyance or conflict.
5. **250 m is operational.** It was retained after matched multiscale validation and is not claimed as a universal species scale.
6. **Transfer validation is conditional.** Formula selection was frozen before whole-station CV but was not repeated inside every fold.
7. **Support labels do not edit predictions.** PID and AOA say where interpretation is supported; Monitoring Gaps are not low activity.
8. **Planning overlap is contextual.** Intersections with mapped places and functions identify screening priorities, not realised human exposure.
9. **Monitoring designs are prospective.** Coverage and uncertainty gains are calculated under a frozen model and still require site feasibility review.
