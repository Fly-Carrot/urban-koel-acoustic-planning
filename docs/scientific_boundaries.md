# Definitions and interpretation

| Quantity or analysis | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Weekly acoustic opportunity, `psi` | Detection-corrected probability of vocal activity during a station-week; not abundance or nesting. |
| Daily detectability, `p` | Probability of retaining a positive sample on a surveyed day, conditional on weekly opportunity. Recording effort and acoustic context enter this layer. |
| Potential-host opportunity | Predicted acoustic co-occurrence used as ecological context; local host use or parasitism was not observed. |
| Sampled-minute exposure, `E` | Probability that a scheduled sampled minute is classified Koel-positive under the stated recording design. It does not measure sound level or perceived disturbance. |
| Landscape scale | 250 m was retained after matched scale comparisons; it is an operational neighbourhood. |
| Whole-station validation | Tests transfer of prespecified formulae. Variable selection was not repeated inside each fold. |
| PID and AOA | Describe reporting context and environmental representation. Support classes accompany predictions without changing their values. Monitoring Gaps mean weak support, not low activity. |
| Planning overlap | Co-location with mapped places and functions; identifies priorities for field assessment. |
| Monitoring candidates | Prospective selections under fixed model rules; deployment still requires access, permission and micro-site checks. |
